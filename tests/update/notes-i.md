## What's new

- Atlas Updater follows the keyboard: tabbing to a button lower on the page scrolls it into view.
- Crash reports from programs outside a package are named by the program, not its full path.
- "Crash report sent" and similar confirmations clear when you move to another section.
- Settings no longer says no crash report server is set up after you turn reports off.
